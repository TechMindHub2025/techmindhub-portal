## Generator prompts and review

All prompts requested a clean humanist sans, exact English phrases, restrained TechMind Hub signature, the registered category palette, separate cover and portrait compositions, no sources or decorative numbered badges on artwork, and no unsourced metrics or fabricated apparatus.

1. **Rubicon cover:** 16:9 light blue-gray editorial illustration, dense independent Git branch trajectories reaching a small reference switch with a separate broad immutable pack field beneath. **Inner:** vertical exploded architecture with named immutable packs, Blob Storage, Ref-CAS holding refs and pack manifest, replaceable virtual replicas and background compaction. Upload the pack before the metadata transaction. No GitHub/Azure logo or real deployment claim.
2. **Photonics cover:** 16:9 pale cyan scientific material close-up, TFLN waveguide and luminous microring, short write pulse and retained optical state. **Inner:** vertical conceptual microring and cross-section of spatially distinct oxygen-doped/defect-rich regions; separate optical write and read paths, no electrical circuit, microscopy claim or fabricated spectral curve.
3. **Prometheus cover:** 16:9 pale violet resource landscape, a query touching many time-series branches with a budget boundary. **Inner:** vertical narrow/wide scopes with three named stages, `Estimate cost`, `Execute within limit`, `Report actual cost`; exact feature flag and explicit no-speedup caveat. The first inner candidate invented an invalid PromQL example and was discarded; final art has no query code.
4. **OTel cover:** 16:9 violet telemetry glyph ribbon changing shape across an amber upgrade seam, with no version or language labels on the cover. **Inner:** vertical separate vignettes: opt-in PHP and Ruby instrumentation on top, Node.js automatic upgrade blocked across 0.79.0 below; human reviews conventions and dashboards, separate RBAC note. Two earlier attempts connected PHP/Ruby to the Node.js boundary or invented version pairs and were discarded.
