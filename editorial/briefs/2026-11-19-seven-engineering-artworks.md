# Seven engineering analyses: paired artworks, November 19, 2026

Seven separate 16:9 covers and 4:5 article infographics follow the existing TechMind Hub editorial visual canon. They are conceptual, not product photographs, literal circuit diagrams or measured charts. Each cover uses short legible display text and a small TechMind Hub signature. No third-party marks or quantitative claims appear in the artwork. The infographics explain the particular engineering question, with concise labels. The article prose and cited primary sources supply all measured values and qualifications.

| Article | Domain | Cover motif | Infographic argument |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| OpenAI chip design | AI | Oblique die with vector lanes and matrix array | RTL, equivalence checking and physical layout |
| Java4Shell | Digital trust | Malformed input crossing an edge filter toward runtime memory | HTTP coverage versus other routes; patch runtime |
| Pelagos | Photonics | Error patterns distributed over GPU islands | Sampled errors, decoder evaluation and statistical output |
| CDU fluid monitoring | Infrastructure | Coolant loop and sampling window | Cold plates, heat transfer and fluid chemistry |
| Vodafone AI-RAN | Infrastructure | Málaga lab, radio sector and edge compute | AI for RAN, shared platform and edge applications |
| Codex sandbox | Digital trust | Isolated workspace and gated network aperture | File and network permission boundaries |
| Nuclear site selection | Energy and environment | Topographical screening map | Geographic, environmental and grid layers, then human review |

The seven motifs deliberately vary in viewpoint, density and spatial structure while using domain accents from `src/data/editorial-domains.json`. The chip and quantum images depict research workflows, not claims of entire-processor gains or real-time error correction. The Vodafone image depicts a test hub, not a deployed commercial AI-RAN. The nuclear image depicts screening, not a commissioned reactor. Original PNGs are retained separately; only the prescribed WebP/JPEG derivatives are uploaded to R2.
