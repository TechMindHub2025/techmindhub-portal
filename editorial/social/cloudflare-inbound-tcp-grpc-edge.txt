Cloudflare has opened inbound TCP for Workers and bidirectional gRPC in Containers as a public beta. Its example forwards a stream from Spectrum through a Worker and Durable Object to a container, where TLS terminates and the gRPC server runs.

That opens new options for long-lived edge services, including real-time voice. It also makes connection lifecycle, backpressure and failure recovery central design questions. Public beta and per-zone port limits matter when evaluating a deployment.
