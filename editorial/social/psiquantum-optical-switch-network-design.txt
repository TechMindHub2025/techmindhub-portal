PsiQuantum reports an 8×8 optical circuit-switching demonstrator with typical laboratory insertion loss of 1.2 dB and reconfiguration in less than three milliseconds. Samples are going to selected prospective customers; this is not a deployed AI-cluster result.

Reconfiguration time is not the delay of every packet on an established path. Our analysis asks when a circuit is worth setting up, how a scheduler handles changing traffic and what the full optical link loses beyond one switch.
