A pump's maximum flow is not a rack's cooling capacity.

Gates has introduced the ThermalCool 2100SS, a 4U-compatible liquid-cooling pump designed for rack CDU integration. Its published limits include up to 200 L/min, 3 bar differential pressure, 48 VDC input and a 2.1 kW motor. Global availability is expected in early 2027.

The useful engineering question is what happens at the actual duty point. Flow must be evaluated against circuit pressure losses; heat transport also depends on coolant properties and temperature rise. Using water for illustration, 200 L/min could carry roughly 70 kW at a 5°C temperature rise. That is not a certified CDU capacity.

The integrated liquid-cooled motor also transfers its own heat into the circuit. Parallel pump capability makes N+1 possible, but valves, controls, duty sizing and failover tests determine whether redundancy actually works.

Our analysis unpacks the specifications without treating a product announcement as field evidence.
