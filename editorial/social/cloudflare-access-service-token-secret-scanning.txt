GitHub Secret Scanning now recognizes the Cloudflare Access service token secret type. This adds visibility into one class of machine credential accidentally committed to a repository.

A detection is the beginning of a response. Teams still need to confirm the token's scope and usage, replace it in legitimate services, revoke the exposed value and check access history. The new detector does not automatically revoke a credential or cover every Cloudflare secret.

Our analysis examines the boundary between pattern recognition and the operational work of managing machine identities.
