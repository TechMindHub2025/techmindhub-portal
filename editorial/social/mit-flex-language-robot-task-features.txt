“Keep coffee away from the laptop” might need to mean “keep liquid away from electronics.” MIT and NYU's FLEX method edits the features a robot uses to represent a task: add missing context, generalize a category, or mask an irrelevant detail.

The method has been studied in navigation and tabletop manipulation, including physical robot demonstrations. It does not prove that every language-derived constraint is correct. We examine how a corrected instruction becomes a revised plan and where verification remains essential.
