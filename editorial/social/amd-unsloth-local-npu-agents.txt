A local NPU path for AI agents.

AMD and Unsloth connected Unsloth Desktop to XDNA 2 NPUs through Lemonade and FastFlowLM. More than 100 compatible applications can call the local API; their entire workflows do not run on the NPU. Model support, memory, latency and energy per completed task will determine practical value.
