How can rescue robotics researchers compare navigation systems when every team builds a different machine?

SPARC proposes a developing RoboCup Rescue platform based on the Unitree A2-W. Its initial resources include 2026 competition datasets, an open navigation stack and early simulation assets. A more common base could help isolate the effects of perception, mapping and planning methods.

The standard is still forming, including its manipulation setup and rules. Repeatable obstacle-course performance also does not establish reliability in a real disaster. Our analysis examines what shared hardware can clarify, and what it cannot.
