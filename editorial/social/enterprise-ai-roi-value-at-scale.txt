AI adoption is easier to count than enterprise value.

In a July 2026 IDC survey of 5,204 leaders commissioned by Microsoft, 66% reported substantial or transformational AI results. Yet 48% said they were scaling value across business units. The reported average AI return was 4× among the most mature group versus 2.7× among slow adopters.

Those are executive assessments, not audited returns or proof that maturity caused the difference. The more useful question is how a local pilot becomes a repeatable workflow with a baseline, implementation costs, quality checks and human accountability.

Our analysis examines that measurement gap and the governance needed when AI crosses team boundaries.
