Trinity Capital committed $100 million in debt financing to Phononic to expand solid-state cooling production for AI infrastructure. Phononic targets localized hotspots in GPUs, HBM, networking chips and optics.

A thermoelectric module moves heat by consuming electricity; its hot side still needs to reject the heat it moves plus its own input power. Financing signals a manufacturing bet, not measured superiority over other cooling designs. The useful comparison needs operating-temperature data, electrical draw, reliability and system-level economics.
