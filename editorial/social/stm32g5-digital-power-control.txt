STM32G5: The Controller Between AI Compute and Its Power Supply

AI accelerators get the attention. Their power converters must still follow changing loads, manage switching events and respond to faults.

STMicroelectronics announced its STM32G5 family on November 4. It combines a 280 MHz Cortex-M33 with upgraded timing and mixed-signal peripherals. ST claims twice the compute performance of STM32G4 and points to data-centre supplies and robotic motors. That is a controller comparison, not a measured twofold efficiency improvement.

Our engineering analysis follows the complete converter: sensing quality, feedback timing, SiC/GaN switching trade-offs, protection, load-range losses and serviceability. A useful evaluation compares the same power stage and workload under controlled conditions.
