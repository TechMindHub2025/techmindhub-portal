A local AI model may fit on a device yet compete with the sensors that make its decisions useful. EdgePace proposes pausing inference around foreground deadlines and observation freshness.

In one OnePlus 13 experiment, the authors report a drop in missed camera deadlines from 66.78% to 5.83% while retaining 90.4% of model throughput. This is a preprint result for a specified workload. Our analysis asks how to test timely decisions and handle remaining misses.
