A data center can have grid capacity and still struggle at the final electrical distribution stages.

In a Fluke-commissioned survey of 1,300 professionals across six countries, 78% reported electrical issues contributing to disruption, delay, downtime or near misses in the previous year. Only 33% considered their infrastructure fully ready for high-density AI loads.

These are self-reported responses, not a measured global outage rate. We examine how power-quality measurements, event logs and load transitions can turn a broad concern into a testable diagnosis.
