Google and Mandiant describe 2026 cases in which stolen cloud identities or exposed model endpoints were converted into billable AI usage. Some investigated victims accumulated more than $100,000 of fraudulent consumption in a day; intermediaries called LLM Mixers resold unauthorized access.

The engineering response joins identity, resource permissions and cost telemetry. Limit which service accounts can create keys or provision capacity, set workload-specific quotas and test whether an alert can lead to containment before spending grows. Rotating one key is insufficient if an attacker has created others.

The reported costs are from particular investigations, not a typical loss estimate.
