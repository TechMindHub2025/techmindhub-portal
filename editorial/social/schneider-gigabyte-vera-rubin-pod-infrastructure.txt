An AI factory cannot be engineered as a collection of independently specified racks, CDUs and power modules.

Schneider Electric and Giga Computing, a GIGABYTE subsidiary, are working on a reference architecture around NVIDIA Vera Rubin NVL72. The important question is what happens where compute meets the facility: electrical input, liquid flow and temperature, residual air heat, monitoring and failure boundaries.

A repeatable POD should have one agreed operating envelope. Separate equipment nameplates do not prove that the combined system can survive a pump failure, power transfer or controls outage at full requested load.

This is a proposed architecture, not a measured deployment result. The analysis examines the commissioning evidence an operator would need before treating a blueprint as a reliable building block.
