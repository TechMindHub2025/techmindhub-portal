Penn State has expanded shared research equipment for semiconductor film growth, metal deposition and atomic-force microscopy. The facilities opened in October and were reported in November, with roughly $4.3 million in support.

A shorter loop from material growth to contact formation and measurement can aid investigation of 2D and wide-bandgap devices. It does not itself demonstrate manufacturing yield. What repeatability result would persuade you that a lab process is ready to scale?
