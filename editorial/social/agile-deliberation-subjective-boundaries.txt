Agile Deliberation: Defining a Category with Its Difficult Cases

An image classifier for “healthy food” needs a definition. Easy positive examples rarely reveal where that definition breaks down. Google Research's Agile Deliberation asks a reviewer to judge borderline images, then uses the feedback to refine a candidate classifier.

The authors report 18 interactive sessions across two subjective tasks, with better results against selected baselines and less reported effort than manual deliberation. This is a small, task-specific research study. It does not settle whose judgment should define a category or establish a production-ready moderation system.

Our engineering analysis examines reviewer disagreement, held-out evaluation and how to record a change to the category itself.
