AWS STS GetScopedCredentials lets an application derive a narrower temporary session from an existing one. The effective permissions are the intersection of the parent session and a scope-down policy: delegated work cannot receive rights the parent lacks.

This can reduce the authority handed to an AI sub-agent or tenant task without creating a new IAM role for every operation. It still needs restrictive parent roles, a sandbox, short-lived sessions and an audit trail. How do you test that a delegated credential is denied access outside its intended task?
