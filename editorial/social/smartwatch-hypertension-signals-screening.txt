Detecting Hypertension Without a Cuff: AI Learns From Smartwatch Signals

Google Research combined smartwatch optical pulse and accelerometer signals across days to estimate the likelihood of a persistent hypertensive state. In a prospective study of 196 people against 24-hour ambulatory cuff monitoring, initial-screening sensitivity was 65.8% and specificity 90.0%.

The watch did not measure blood pressure directly. Moderate sensitivity means a negative result cannot rule out hypertension; a positive signal calls for conventional clinical assessment.

We examine the difference between 492,347 hours of pretraining data and the smaller clinical validation, as well as the practical questions around signal quality, follow-up and false reassurance.
