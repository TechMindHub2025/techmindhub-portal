STM32H7P: Can Sensing, Inference and Control Share One MCU?

STMicroelectronics' new STM32H7P puts a Cortex-M7, hardware signal processor and Neural-ART NPU in the same industrial controller family.

The company reports up to 85 GOPS and fortyfold faster inference for its specific arc-fault model against its software implementation. Neither figure tells us the full time from sensor acquisition to a safe action. The DSP stage, transfers, memory contention, application logic and worst-case control deadlines all matter.

Our analysis proposes an end-to-end test: timestamp the observation, preserve the model and preprocessing versions, measure latency under concurrent control work, and define what happens when a result arrives too late. Accuracy and thermal behaviour need separate evidence.

OEM samples are available; production is planned for January 2027.
