Computing Without Electronics: Origami Structures Perform Ternary Logic

A folded Kresling structure can carry a stable physical state; a torsional transition can change it. Researchers report a modular architecture with seven logic gates and binary and ternary half- and full-adders.

The hard engineering questions begin when one response must become another module's input. Switching thresholds, repeatability, actuation, readout and circuit depth will determine where mechanical computation is useful.

This is a research platform for programming physical behavior, not a replacement for a modern processor. Our analysis separates the demonstrated gates from the performance and durability evidence still needed.
