More neutral-atom qubit sites require more than an optical array. Control electronics, addressing, readout and system integration must scale together. A US CHIPS R&D award of up to $100 million backs Atom Computing's work on that stack.

The funding ceiling is not a performance result, and tens of thousands of qubits remain a development target. Our analysis separates the control problem from the still-unproven end-to-end fault-tolerant system.
