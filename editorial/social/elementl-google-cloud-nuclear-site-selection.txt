The first bottleneck for a nuclear project may be a defensible shortlist of sites.

Elementl Power and Google Cloud are building Lattice, beginning with Atlas, a tool that screens geographic, environmental and infrastructure characteristics across potential locations. Elementl says preliminary desktop analysis could move from months to days or weeks.

That is a company claim about early research, not a measured reduction in reactor construction time. A useful shortlist still needs traceable data, human engineering judgment, regulatory review and local validation. Our analysis explores where AI-assisted screening can help and what remains unproven.
