Liquid cooling reliability is also a question of what happens to the liquid over time.

Ecolab and CoolIT unveiled the CHx 3000, a 3 MW-rated coolant distribution unit integrated with 3D TRASAR fluid monitoring. The engineering proposition is to observe fluid condition alongside flow and temperature as dense AI systems operate.

A product rating is not a measured result at every site. Independent performance, water-use and long-duration reliability data were not provided in the announcement. We examine the practical evidence operators would need before procurement and deployment.
