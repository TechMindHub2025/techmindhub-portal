SOPS can keep the structure of an encrypted configuration file in version control while controlling who can read its values. It supports a practical workflow: review the fields beside an application change, then edit with an authorised identity.

Our analysis starts with dummy credentials and follows plaintext into deployment. It also separates three tasks that are often conflated: changing recipients, rotating a file's data-encryption key and replacing the service password or token inside it.
