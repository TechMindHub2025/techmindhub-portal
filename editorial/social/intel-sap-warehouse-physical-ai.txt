Intel and SAP demonstrated a warehouse proof of concept linking SAP EWM requests to autonomous robot tasks. The robot moves materials and returns status to the enterprise workflow.

Our analysis focuses on the hard part after dispatch: what happens when an aisle is blocked, a task acknowledgement is lost or a person moves an item manually? Status, exceptions and reconciliation have to close the loop.

The demonstration is a useful architecture example, not evidence of dependable operation or economics across sites.
