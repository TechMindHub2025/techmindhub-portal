A Brotli decoder vulnerability addressed by Node.js on October 21 illustrates how one low-level dependency can travel through several cloud deployment paths. AWS's bulletin, updated through November 3, distinguishes patched managed runtimes from customer-controlled images and pinned build artifacts.

Our engineering assessment: a supplier patch is a procurement event for a fix, not proof that the running workload contains it. Map the decoder to reachable inputs, rebuild containers from corrected base images, move unsupported runtimes and verify deployed digests. A CI image and a production function may need separate remediation.

The vulnerability has potentially serious consequences, but the public notices do not establish widespread exploitation.
