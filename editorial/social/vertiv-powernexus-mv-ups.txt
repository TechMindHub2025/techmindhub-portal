AI data centers face two related electrical challenges: short disturbances from the grid and rapid changes in the load presented to it.

Vertiv's PowerNexus proposal places an outdoor medium-voltage UPS and storage at that boundary. Vendor-hosted demonstrations have explored voltage and load events; shipments are expected in mid-2027.

Our analysis separates fault ride-through from grid-facing load buffering, and asks what a site-specific acceptance test must show. A product demonstration alone cannot establish a faster grid connection for every project.
