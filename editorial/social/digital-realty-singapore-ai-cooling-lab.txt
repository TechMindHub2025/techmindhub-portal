What should be tested before a 150 kW-plus AI rack enters production?

Digital Realty has opened a multi-vendor Innovation Lab in its operating SIN11 data center in Singapore. It lists chilled-water cooling, liquid-to-liquid and liquid-to-air paths, parallel UPS and BMS/EPMS monitoring.

The engineering value lies in checking interfaces: flow and temperature, residual air heat, failover, alarms and service isolation. The supported rack-density figure is not an independent endurance test or a facility PUE result. Our analysis outlines what a meaningful acceptance programme would measure.
