Walmart and Symbotic have expanded their agreement with an initial commitment of about $6 billion over several years for further automation at 25 US regional distribution centres. The announcement is a commitment, not a measured result from completed new installations.

Our analysis follows the goods beyond the machine: receiving, storage, pallet sequencing, shipping and store replenishment. A fast pallet build helps only if damage, unloading and recovery also improve under a comparable workload.
