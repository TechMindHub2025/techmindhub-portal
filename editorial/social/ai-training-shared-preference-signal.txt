OpenAI and Google plan to support a common robots.txt signal for websites' AI-training preferences. The proposed `__usage_ai-training` token could reduce the need for a separate instruction for each participating provider.

Support is still forthcoming: OpenAI targets the coming months and Google 2027. The specification is an individual Internet-Draft, and the signal expresses a use preference rather than blocking crawling.

Our analysis asks what happens after retrieval. A provider needs to carry the observed preference through dataset preparation and training decisions if the shared signal is to be accountable in practice.
