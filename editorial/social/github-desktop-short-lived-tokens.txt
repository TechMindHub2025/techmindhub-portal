GitHub Desktop's Short-Lived Tokens Narrow the Credential Exposure Window

GitHub Desktop 3.6.10 and later supports automatically renewed, short-lived tokens for eligible accounts in a gradual rollout. If one token is copied, its usefulness is bounded by its validity and any revocation. An attacker may still act while it is valid, and a compromised device needs its own investigation.

Existing signed-in sessions do not automatically migrate. The rollout covers github.com and Enterprise Cloud, not Enterprise Server.

Our analysis separates the lifetime of one token from the wider session and workstation trust boundary, and describes what an organization can check without inventing a precise expiry duration.
