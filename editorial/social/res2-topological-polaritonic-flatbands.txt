ReS₂ Metasurfaces: A Directional Flatband for Light and Excitons

A Nature Materials experiment patterns anisotropic rhenium disulfide to create optical modes whose dispersion is nearly flat along one in-plane direction and curved along another. The work also examines their coupling to polarized excitons.

This is a physical demonstration of controllable light–matter interaction, not an optical processor. Our analysis separates characterization and modeling from the device evidence still needed: repeatable fabrication, coupling and loss budgets, bandwidth and integration.
