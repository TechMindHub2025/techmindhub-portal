A training worker can stop waiting for a sandbox while Kubernetes continues creating it. When retries overlap, the system needs to account for late pods and confirm that abandoned ones are removed.

Amazon's Sandman preprint reports a controlled test with 11,858 concurrent tiny sandboxes. That result is specific to its test configuration. The operational lesson is about ownership across cancellation, reconciliation and cleanup.

Our analysis proposes interrupting acquisition at several points, then comparing intended environments with actual resources. The important question is which sandboxes still exist, and why.
