A Nature Energy study identifies one-minute frequency disturbances and measures how synchronized control in UPS and battery systems can make a recurring electrical pulse visible beyond the local installation.

The engineering concern is collective timing. A routine action in one device can matter when many controllers perform it at the same clock boundary. The analysis covers measurement, bounded schedule spreading and the risk of fleets aligning again after restart or resynchronization.

The measured mechanism does not identify every contributor to every grid pattern, and it is not evidence of a cyberattack.
