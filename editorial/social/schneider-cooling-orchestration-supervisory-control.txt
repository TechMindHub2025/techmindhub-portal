Cooling an AI data center is not one control loop.

Cold plates, CDUs, pumps, chillers and heat-rejection equipment respond on different time scales. Schneider Electric's recent technical article makes the case for supervising them together, using rack demand and thermal telemetry to coordinate plant setpoints.

The engineering challenge is safety and accountability. Local protection must remain authoritative, supervisory controls need bounded operating ranges, and equipment must fall back safely when the higher-level controller or its communications fail.

Efficiency claims need consistent energy and water measurements. A lower PUE does not automatically mean a lower WUE, and a vendor explanation is not an independent comparative test.

The new analysis separates what orchestration could improve from what operators still have to validate.
