The most important number on a coolant distribution unit is not always its maximum MW rating.

nVent has announced the modular CX121 CDU with a stated maximum operating performance of 2.3 MW, with production expected late in 2026 and pre-orders underway. Its role is to transfer rack heat across a facility boundary under specified flow and temperature conditions.

We examine parallel modules, common-cause failures and why a design point matters. The separate OCP Brazos liquid-to-air CDU in the same announcement addresses a different retrofit path. Neither product announcement constitutes an independent field test.
