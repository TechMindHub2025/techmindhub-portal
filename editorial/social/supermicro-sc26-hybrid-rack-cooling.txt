Direct-to-chip cooling and rear-door heat exchangers solve different thermal problems.

Supermicro's SC26 portfolio includes in-row and sidecar CDUs as well as rear-door exchangers, supporting a range of AI and HPC configurations. A cold plate collects heat at a processor. A rear-door exchanger removes heat from rack exhaust air. These paths can complement one another, but they are not interchangeable.

For an existing data hall, the decision depends on residual air load, fluid supply temperatures, piping, structural limits and how pumps, fans and controls behave during failures.

The SC26 page describes a technology portfolio, not an independent efficiency comparison or a documented new deployment. The analysis follows the heat from the component to the final facility sink and identifies what would need testing before a retrofit.
