Quantum error correction depends on a large classical research workload: evaluating decoders against many sampled error patterns.

IBM and AMD have released Pelagos, an open-source, GPU-accelerated framework for those studies. Their reported tests reached roughly 7.5× speedup across eight GPUs for the GPU decoding stage relative to one GPU. That ratio does not describe an entire experiment or a quantum computer's performance.

Our analysis separates decoder evaluation from real-time quantum-classical operation and asks what reproducible scaling would need to measure.
